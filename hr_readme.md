# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **employee attrition** to understand why employees leave an organization and identify the key factors associated with employee turnover.

The analysis was performed using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**, covering data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based business analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

---

##  Project Objectives

* Analyze overall employee attrition.
* Identify departments with high attrition.
* Analyze attrition by age, gender, job role, and education.
* Understand the impact of salary and job satisfaction on attrition.
* Analyze employee overtime and work-life factors.
* Identify high-risk employee segments.
* Build an interactive HR analytics dashboard.
* Generate actionable insights for HR decision-making.
